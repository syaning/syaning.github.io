title: 176. Second Highest Salary
tags: Medium Database
---

[View on LeetCode](https://leetcode.com/problems/second-highest-salary/)

**Approach:** Second max = max of salaries excluding the overall max (via NOT IN subquery).

**Complexity:** O(n) time with two scans (DB-dependent)

::: code-group

```sql
select max(Salary) from Employee where Salary not in (select max(Salary) from Employee)
```

:::
